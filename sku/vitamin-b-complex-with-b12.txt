Noice Vitamin B-Complex with B12 (product code 8905127401199)

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Noice Vitamin B-Complex with B12 is a daily health supplement that provides all eight B vitamins in a single tablet, with vitamin B12 in its active form, methylcobalamin. It is designed for adults who want to support their daily intake of B vitamins, particularly people who eat little or no meat, since vitamin B12 is found mainly in animal foods.

FORMAT AND PACK
Tablet, supplied in a bottle of 60 tablets. Serving size is 1 tablet. Category: Health Supplement. Not for medicinal use.

KEY FEATURES
All eight B vitamins: B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, biotin, folate and B12.
Active B12: methylcobalamin, a form the body can use directly.
Active folate: L-methylfolate, a form the body does not need to convert.
Vegetarian and gluten free.
One tablet a day.

ACTIVE INGREDIENTS AND THEIR ROLES
Exact amounts per tablet are in the supplement facts. The forms used and their general roles are:
Vitamin B1, as thiamine mononitrate: helps the body convert carbohydrates into energy and supports normal nerve function.
Vitamin B2, as riboflavin: helps release energy from food. Riboflavin is naturally bright yellow.
Vitamin B3, as niacinamide: contributes to normal energy metabolism and normal skin. Niacinamide does not cause the skin flushing associated with plain niacin.
Vitamin B5, as calcium D-pantothenate: helps the body release energy from food.
Vitamin B6, as pyridoxine hydrochloride: supports protein metabolism and normal nervous system function.
Biotin, as D-biotin: supports normal energy metabolism and normal hair and skin.
Folate, as L-methylfolate: supports normal cell division and red blood cell formation.
Vitamin B12, as methylcobalamin: supports normal red blood cell formation and normal nerve function.
RDA percentages are not printed on this label.

OTHER INGREDIENTS
Microcrystalline cellulose, dicalcium phosphate, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose (tablet coating), magnesium stearate (vegetable source), silicon dioxide.

DIETARY AND ALLERGEN INFORMATION
Vegetarian, carrying the green vegetarian symbol. Gluten free.
Contains no gelatin, dairy, egg, soy, nuts, fish or shellfish.
Contains no added sugar, sweeteners, artificial colours or artificial flavours.
The product is not certified vegan.
It is manufactured in a facility that may also handle other allergens.

DIRECTIONS FOR USE
Take 1 tablet daily after breakfast, swallowed whole with water.
Taking it after a meal helps avoid the mild nausea some people feel when taking B vitamins on an empty stomach.
Take it at about the same time each day.
Do not exceed the recommended daily usage of 1 tablet.
Missed a dose: take the next tablet at the usual time the following day. Do not take two tablets to make up for a missed one.

STORAGE
Store in a cool, dry place below 30°C, away from direct sunlight.
Keep the bottle tightly closed and out of reach of children.
Do not use after the expiry date printed on the pack.

PRECAUTIONS
Pregnant or breastfeeding women, children, and persons on medication should consult a doctor before use.
People who already take a multivitamin or another B vitamin supplement should check both labels and consult a doctor or pharmacist, to avoid taking the same vitamins twice.
Not for medicinal use. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
If more than the recommended amount has been taken, or a child has swallowed tablets, contact a doctor or call 112 immediately.
If any unusual symptoms appear after taking it, stop use and consult a doctor.

COMMON QUESTIONS
Bright yellow urine: this is expected. It comes from riboflavin (vitamin B2), which is naturally yellow. The body uses what it needs and passes out the excess. It is harmless.
Energy: B vitamins help the body turn food into energy, but they are not stimulants like caffeine and do not give an instant energy boost.
Vegetarian diets: vitamin B12 is found mainly in animal foods, which is why people who eat little or no meat may look to a supplement for it.
Taking it with tea or coffee: the label does not give specific guidance. Taking it after breakfast, as directed, is the recommended routine.
