Noice Omega-3 Fish Oil (product code DEMO-0003)

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Noice Omega-3 Fish Oil is an imaginary product made up for this demo. It is described as a daily health supplement that provides the omega-3 fatty acids EPA and DHA from fish oil.

FORMAT AND PACK
Softgel, supplied in a bottle of 60 softgels. Serving size is 1 softgel. Category: Health Supplement. Not for medicinal use.

DIETARY AND ALLERGEN INFORMATION
Contains fish. The softgel shell contains gelatin. Not suitable for vegetarians.

DIRECTIONS FOR USE
Take 1 softgel daily with a meal, swallowed whole with water.
Do not exceed the recommended daily usage of 1 softgel.

STORAGE
Store in a cool, dry place below 25°C, away from direct sunlight.
Keep the bottle tightly closed and out of reach of children.

PRECAUTIONS
Pregnant or breastfeeding women, children, persons on blood-thinning medicines and persons on any other medication should consult a doctor before use.
Do not use if allergic to fish.
Not for medicinal use. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
If more than the recommended amount has been taken, or a child has swallowed softgels, contact a doctor or call 112 immediately.
